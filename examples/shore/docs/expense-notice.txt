Travel reimbursement deadline: File travel reimbursement within 14 calendar days after return.
Travel reimbursement evidence: Attach the itemized receipt and approved trip reference.
Local rail fare: Claim the standard class ticket price for approved journeys.
Taxi approval: Obtain written supervisor approval before booking a taxi.
Meal allowance: The overnight meal allowance is 28 credits per person.
Expense correction: Reopen a returned claim within five working days.
Mileage rate: Approved private vehicle travel earns 0.42 credits per kilometer.
Missing receipt: Attach a signed loss declaration to the expense claim.
