Shift swap notice: Submit swap requests at least 36 hours before the affected shift.
Shift swap approval: Both employees and the roster supervisor must confirm in the roster portal.
Annual leave notice: File annual leave requests at least 10 calendar days before departure.
Annual leave approver: The department lead approves annual leave in the leave portal.
Emergency leave contact: Call the duty coordinator before the scheduled shift begins.
Shift swap record: Keep the roster portal confirmation until the following payroll cycle closes.
Training leave allowance: Staff may request two paid training days each calendar year.
Returning from leave: Confirm the next rostered shift with the duty coordinator.
