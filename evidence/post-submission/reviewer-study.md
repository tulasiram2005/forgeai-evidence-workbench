# Reviewer pilot protocol

Decision: do hybrid evidence cards help a person reach a correct, cited decision on an unfamiliar document collection? Run this pilot before changing the submission default.

## Materials and assignment

Use a fresh collection from one consenting team, with its original files and normal search interface. A domain owner writes actual questions before seeing either system, including questions whose subject exists but whose requested fact is absent. A second domain reviewer records all required facts, valid supporting passages, and acceptable missing-information decisions before sessions start. Resolve disagreements and hash the question set and rubric. Use only documents approved for local evaluation. No model or ranking changes during the pilot.

Assign reviewers to counterbalanced task lists. Every task is attempted in both conditions by different people. No participant repeats a question. In both conditions allow access to all source files and identical search. In the treatment add up to three frozen hybrid passages, each explicitly labeled unconfirmed, with a way to open its source. Hide expected answers and condition labels from graders. Record technical failures and timeouts alongside completed tasks.

## Outcome and decision

Primary outcome: correct decision with all required facts and an authentic supporting passage, or a correct explicit unsupported decision. Report supported-case success and near-miss false assertions separately. Record elapsed time to submission, timeout count, and median time among correct decisions as secondary outcomes. Show denominators by reviewer and task. A faster wrong answer is a failure. Do not infer a speed benefit from a difference in the mix of questions completed correctly.

Before collecting responses, agree with the domain owner on a material improvement, tolerated false-assertion difference, maximum task time, participant count, and analysis of uncertainty accounting for shared reviewers and tasks. Freeze those choices before starting. Use the findings for a limited pilot decision only. Record negative results and inspect cards that led reviewers toward unsupported claims.

## Open dependency

Requires consenting reviewers and previously unseen documents and questions. No human sessions or observed time savings have been recorded.
